Everything that came in, edited down to what matters today.

Every morning, Daily Focus uses AI to read your mail, calendar, pull requests, tickets and chat. It leaves out the noise, ranks what’s left against the goal you’re working toward, and puts the few things that must move today at the top.

What it does with your day.

It runs on your computer
Daily Focus is an app on your own computer. Your day leaves it only through the AI coding tool you chose, Claude Code or Codex.
It uses the AI subscription you already pay for
Daily Focus runs Claude Code on any paid Claude plan, or Codex on ChatGPT Plus or higher, signed in as you. It has no account or subscription of its own.
It says what it couldn’t read
When a source fails, the morning’s report names it. A calendar it couldn’t reach never passes for a quiet day.
You can read every instruction
It’s MIT licensed, and if you’re curious how the agents decide what to keep, their prompts are in the repository.

Install it on your computer.

  • From a terminal

    npx daily-focus

    Opens the dashboard in your browser, on a setup screen that asks a few questions, one at a time, with the likely answer already ticked. Your AI coding tool looks into the rest, like which calendars are work ones. Needs Node.js 22.18 or newer.

  • On a Mac

    Download for Mac

    A menu bar app that starts the dashboard at login, shows how old the brief is, and tells you when a new one arrives. Node.js comes with it. Needs Apple silicon and macOS 13.5 or newer.

Either way, you need Claude Code on a paid Claude plan, or Codex on ChatGPT Plus or higher. GitHub, Jira and the macOS calendar are optional. The setup guide covers each step. To look around on invented data first, run npx daily-focus --demo.
