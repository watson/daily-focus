Start the day knowing what comes first.

Every morning, Daily Focus has Claude Code or Codex read your calendar, mail, pull requests and tickets, then write a short brief that ranks the day against the one thing you’re trying to finish. It runs on your own computer, in a tab you keep pinned.

    What it does with your day.

    It runs on your computer
    Daily Focus is an app on your own computer. Your day leaves it only through the AI coding tool you chose, Claude Code or Codex.
    It uses the AI subscription you already pay for
    Daily Focus runs Claude Code or Codex, signed in as you on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan. It has no account or subscription of its own.
    It reads, and it drafts
    The morning agent and the assistant read what they need and hand you the result: a ranked brief, an answer, or a Gmail draft waiting for you to send.
    You can read every instruction
    It’s MIT licensed, and if you’re curious what the agents are told to do, their prompts are in the repository.

    Try it on sample data first.

    • Look around

      npx daily-focus --demo

      A morning’s brief, boards and assistant answers, all invented, in a store that goes away when you press Ctrl+C. Add --profile personal to see the version that briefs home, family and paperwork.

    • Use your own day

      npx daily-focus

      Opens a setup screen that asks a few questions, one at a time, with the likely answer already ticked. Your AI coding tool looks into the rest, like which calendars are work ones.

    • Keep it in the menu bar

      Download for Mac

      Starts the dashboard at login, shows how old the brief is, and tells you when a new one arrives. Node.js comes with it. Needs Apple silicon and macOS 13.5 or newer.

    The commands need Node.js 22.18 or newer. Every way needs Claude Code or Codex, signed in. GitHub, Jira and the macOS calendar are optional. The setup guide covers each step.
