This help

Done, snoozed and dismissed all tell the morning agent to leave an item out of tomorrow's brief. A note is sent along with it as free text. On both boards only snooze and note apply: a pull request stays until it's merged or closed, and a ticket whose status looks wrong is put right in Jira rather than here.
